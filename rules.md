# Schwimmen (Thirty-One) — Rules

## Overview

**Schwimmen** (also known as *Thirty-One*, *Knack*, *Schnauz*, *Blitz*, or *Scat*) is a card game of luck and strategy for **2–9 players**. Players swap cards to build the highest-scoring hand, trying to avoid being the player with the lowest score at the end of each round.

---

## Components

- **1 standard deck of 32 cards** (7, 8, 9, 10, Jack, Queen, King, Ace in each of the four suits)
- **3 tokens per player** (coins, matches, chips, etc.) representing lives

---

## Card Values

| Card  | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Ace   | 11    |
| King  | 10    |
| Queen | 10    |
| Jack  | 10    |
| 10    | 10    |
| 9     | 9     |
| 8     | 8     |
| 7     | 7     |

---

## Scoring Combinations

There are **two ways** to form a scoring hand:

### 1. Same Suit
Add up the point values of all cards **sharing the same suit**. Only the highest single-suit total counts. The maximum is **31 points** (e.g., Ace + King + 10 of the same suit).

### 2. Three of a Kind
Three cards of the **same rank** (e.g., three Queens) from different suits score **30½ points**.

### Scoring Examples

| Hand                              | Score  | Explanation                                                   |
|-----------------------------------|--------|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| Queen of Hearts, 10 of Hearts, Jack of Diamonds | 20     | Only the two Hearts count: 10 + 10                            |
| Ace of Clubs, King of Clubs, 9 of Clubs          | 30     | All same suit: 11 + 10 + 9                                    |
| 7 of Diamonds, 7 of Hearts, 7 of Spades          | 30½    | Three of a kind                                               |
| Ace of Diamonds, King of Diamonds, 10 of Diamonds | 31     | All same suit: 11 + 10 + 10 — the best possible hand!        |

---

## Setup

1. Each player receives **3 life tokens**.
2. The dealer deals **3 face-down cards** to each player.
3. The dealer deals themselves **two separate sets of 3 cards**.
4. The dealer looks at the first set and decides:
   - **Keep it:** Place the second set face-up in the center of the table as the market.
   - **Reject it:** Place the first set face-up in the center as the market, and pick up the second set. The remaining cards are set aside as a draw pile.

---

## Gameplay

Play proceeds **clockwise**, starting with the player to the dealer's left (forehand). On their turn, a player **must** choose one of the following actions:

### 1. Swap Cards
Exchange **exactly one** or **all three** cards from your hand with cards from the center market. You may **not** swap exactly two.

### 2. Pass (Push)
Announce **"I pass"** and make no exchange. *(Note: Some variants do not allow passing — agree on this before playing.)*

### 3. Knock (Close)
Announce **"I knock"** (or tap the table). This signals the final round: every other player gets **one more turn**, then the round ends.

---

## End of a Round

A round ends in one of two ways:

1. **A player knocks:** All other players take one final turn, then hands are revealed.
2. **A player holds 31 points (or Fire/Blitz):** That player immediately reveals their hand, and the round ends on the spot — no further turns are taken. This can happen right after the deal.

### If All Players Pass Consecutively
The three market cards are discarded and **three new cards** are drawn from the pile and placed face-up. The player who started the chain of passes takes the next turn.

---

## Determining Losers

After a round ends, all players reveal their hands. The player(s) with the **lowest score lose one life** and place a token in the center of the table.

---

## Lives & Elimination

| Lives Remaining | Status |
|-----------------|--------|
| 3, 2, or 1      | Normal play |
| 0               | **Swimming** — the player may continue but is on their last chance |
| Lost while swimming | **Eliminated** — the player is out of the game |

"Swimming" effectively acts as a **fourth life**. A player who has lost all three tokens keeps playing, but if they lose again, they are eliminated.

---

## Winning the Game

The game continues round after round as players are eliminated. The **last player remaining** is the overall winner.

---

## Variant: Fire / Blitz 🔥

A hand of **three Aces** is called **Fire** (or *Blitz*). It is considered the highest possible hand, beating even 31 points.

- The player **immediately reveals** their hand, and the round ends instantly.
- **All other players lose one life** (not just the lowest scorer).

---

## Optional Rules

Agree on these **before** starting the game:

| Variant | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Extended losers** | All players with 20 or fewer points lose a life, *plus* the player(s) with the lowest score above 20. The round winner never loses a life, even if they are the only one above 20. |
| **Tearing (Reißen)** | A tactical play: if you hold 21 points and can't meaningfully improve, you may deliberately worsen your hand so that another player also loses a life. |
| **30½ tiebreaker** | When multiple players have three-of-a-kind (30½), either they are all equal, or the player with the higher-ranked cards wins/doesn't lose. |
| **No passing** | Passing is not allowed; players must either swap or knock. |
| **Larger deck** | For more players, use a 52-card Whist deck. In Switzerland, a 36-card Jass deck is used and play goes counter-clockwise. |
| **Hidden deal variant** | The player left of the dealer receives 5 cards (3, then 2), keeps 3, and passes 2 face-down to the left. Each subsequent player does the same. |
| **Swimming restriction** | Only the **first** player to lose all 3 lives may swim; subsequent players who lose all lives are eliminated immediately. |

---

## Quick Reference

- **Best hand:** 31 (Ace + two 10-value cards, same suit) or Fire (three Aces)
- **Three of a kind:** 30½ points
- **Only one suit counts** when adding card values
- **Swap 1 or 3 cards** — never exactly 2
- **Knock** = one more turn for everyone else
- **31 or Fire** = round ends immediately
- **3 lives → swimming → eliminated**
